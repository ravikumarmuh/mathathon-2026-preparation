# AI-Assisted Mathematical Research

## Main question

How can frontier AI systems contribute to mathematics beyond simply
producing candidate proofs?

We are particularly interested in the distinction between:

$$
\text{proof generation}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\text{mathematical understanding}.
$$

## Questions we want to investigate

- Can AI discover useful intermediate structures?
- Can it suggest new lemmas or abstractions?
- Can it connect apparently unrelated theories?
- Can it identify useful examples or counterexamples?
- Can it reveal why a proof works rather than merely produce the proof?
- What kinds of AI-generated arguments can humans reliably verify?
