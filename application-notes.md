## Possible team narrative

We are interested in using AI not merely as a black-box theorem prover,
but as a tool for mathematical exploration:

$$
\text{question}
\to
\text{human strategy}
\to
\text{AI exploration}
\to
\text{verification}
\to
\text{mathematical understanding}.
$$
